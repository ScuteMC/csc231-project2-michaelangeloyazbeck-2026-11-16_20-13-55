# Playtest Notes — Vesper: Twilight Courier (Level 1)

**Testers**: Lab peer review (2 classmates) + developer full-level verification playtest on Windows 64-bit build.

## Issues Observed During Testing
1. **Mid-Glide Updraft Drop-Through Bug**: Entering a rising thermal updraft column while *already gliding downward* failed to lift Vesper reliably because Unity's `gravityScale = 3.2f` fought upward acceleration (`32f`) and fast downward entry occasionally skipped `OnTriggerEnter2D`.
2. **Stiff 2-Frame Walk/Run & "Unexplained" Level Props**: Vesper's run animation recycled two similar forward-stride silhouettes, and mid-air updraft wind columns, crumbling perches, and moving-platform tracks looked detached rather than physically anchored to the world.
3. **Zone 1 Glide Gap Intimidation & Overlap Bugs**: First-time testers dropped straight into an empty gap before learning Shadow-Cloak Glide, while early overlapping safety platforms and exposed static spikes felt accidental.
4. **Menu Blandness & Missing Story/Goal Context**: Basic solid-color menus lacked thematic polish and failed to explain why Vesper was collecting shards or what the glowing air vents/spikes did until mid-level.

## Changes Made
1. **Fixed Updraft-While-Gliding Physics (`UpdraftVent.cs` & `PlayerController.cs`)**: Added `OnTriggerStay2D` plus a live `Physics2D.OverlapBox` trigger scan every physics step, suspended `rb.gravityScale = 0f` inside active updrafts, raised `updraftAcceleration` to `58f`, and added a crisp upward catch boost when entering mid-descent.
2. **True 8-Frame Articulated Run Animation (`Vesper_Run_01..08.png`)**: Built a complete 8-keyframe stride cycle (Contact -> Down -> Passing -> Drive/Apex for both right & left steps) with articulated legs, heeled courier boots, pumping gauntlets, shoulder capelet overlap, and flowing scarf ribbons.
3. **100% Grounded, Intentional Level Architecture (`LevelBuilder.cs`)**: Anchored every updraft in either a flush **Rooftop Thermal Vent (`Prop_UpdraftVent`)** or a **Canal Steam Chimney (`Prop_SteamChimney` + `Prop_ChimneyCap`)** descending into the animated **Twilight Canal**, framed crumbling bridges with **Broken Arch Corbels (`Prop_BrokenArch`)** and a ruined watchtower pillar, mounted **Brass Track Pylons (`Prop_TrackPylon`)** & canal gantry piers on every moving platform, dressed districts with wrought-iron railings, streetlamps, and District Gatehouse Arches, and enforced zero overlaps via `ValidateNoPlatformOverlaps()` (21/21 footprints passing).
4. **Ground-Embedded Pop-Up Spikes (`PopUpSpikes.cs`) & Animated Canal Water (`WaterCanal.cs`)**: Concealed spikes inside flush stone floor grates (`Tile_SpikeSlot`) via Unity `SpriteMask` with `ProximityAmbush` spark telegraphs and staggered `TimedCycle` eruptions above a 6-frame moonlit canal water system with mist & splash particles.
5. **Ornate Gothic UI, Story Prologue & Visual Field Guide (`UIManager.cs`, `GameManager.cs`)**: Replaced flat UI boxes with 9-sliced ornate gold/cyan Gothic artwork (`Assets/Art/UI/`), added a 3-card illustrated **Story Prologue**, a dedicated **Story & Mission** screen, a 6-card sprite-and-keycap **Visual Tutorial**, live HUD District Objectives + Sanctum Beacon Progress %, and a **Dawn Restored** S/A/B Courier Rank epilogue.
